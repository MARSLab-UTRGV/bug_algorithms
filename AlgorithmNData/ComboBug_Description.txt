ComboBug Algorithm

Summary: Is a combination of the styles from Bug0 (Common-Sense) and Bug2 (M-Line). 

1. The robot will initialize and record the m-line. Continues to step 2.
2. The robot moves towards the target.
3. If the robot encounters an obstacle it record the coordinates where it hit the obstacle and 
   will start step 4 (Wall Following). Else it will keep moving towards the target.
4. The robot will check if the pathway to the target:
      a. If free and closer than hit point: the robot will move towards the target
	 and continue to step 2.
      b. If free and on m-line: the robot will move towards the target on the m-line
         and continue to step 2.
      c. Else: repeat step 4 until the robot reached the target.
