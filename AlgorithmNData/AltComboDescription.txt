AltCombo Algorithm

Summary: Is a combination of the style of ComboBug and Rev1 and Rev2. Enhancing ComboBug using
directional switch methods similar to the Rev algorithms.

1. The robot will initialize and record the m-line and set the wall following status (This will
   record which method is being used at the moment and always starts with common-sense.) and the
   turn status (which starts at CW)
   Continues to step 2.
2. The robot moves towards the target.
3. If the robot encounters an obstacle it record the coordinates where it hit the obstacle and 
   will start step 4 (Wall Following). Else it will keep moving towards the target.
4. The robot will follow along the wall based on the turn status and check if the pathway to the
   target is free and closer than hit point. Then it will check:
      a. If wall following status is common-sense: the robot will move towards the target,
	 change the wall following status to m-line and the turn-status to CCW. Continue to 
         step 2.
      b. If wall following status is m-line and is on m-line: the robot will move towards the 
         target on the m-line, change the wall following status to common-sense and the turn 
         status to CW. Continue to step 2.
      c. Else: repeat step 4 until the robot reached the target.
